\section*{Use of Agentic Systems and AI-Assistance Disclosure}

This paper, its appendix and the code it reports on relied substantively on AI assistance, disclosed here as the journal's policy asks: assistance, not authorship.
The author is a single human person, named in the byline, who bears full responsibility for content, scope, correctness and editorial judgement; every mistake that remains is the author's.

\emph{Tools, and how each was gated.}
Anthropic's Claude, the Opus and Fable models through the Claude Code CLI, did most of the work, including cold reads of the draft in sessions without the writing session's context.
It worked largely autonomously, in issues, pull requests, slices and commits, in an environment made deliberately constrained and antagonistic: strong type checking, substantial test coverage, formatters, Doxygen and the linter of Section~\ref{sec:limits}; every pull request was gated by continuous integration and by review, usually one by the author and several by Copilot, and merged by hand.
GitHub Copilot's reviewer was used for pull-request review only, in the deepest mode available, with the draft paper as context; its findings on the metatheory of Appendix~\ref{app:fjlt} led to the declaration order, the restriction to total primitives, the small-step evaluation and the present order of the proofs.
Google's Gemini answered quick questions of the research kind, proofread, and read the appendix's proof sketches; its reviews needed heavy discounting, gave a few serendipitous finds, and were fed back to Claude with the author's opinion of them.
The bibliography was used to attune the context and as a source of authority, and builds were offloaded to continuous integration to keep them reproducible, visible and on the record.

\emph{Modes.}
The assistants were used in agreement, to help structure a hypothesis, and in opposition, asked to refute or weaken one; the cold reads, the review threads and the second opinions were of the second kind, and the author decided what survived them.

\emph{Tasks and places.}
Draft generation of prose, listings, tables and figures; the appendix's statements and proofs, drafted from the design decisions recorded in discussion and revised by the author; the test that holds the listings; verification of every citation against its source; and responses in review threads.
Every section and the appendix: no part was written without assistance, and none accepted without the author's reading.
The repository~\cite{Krautler_dedekind_2026} and its pull requests hold the record: commit messages, review threads and, while GitHub keeps them, build logs.
